## Jordan Social Security pension calculator — built 2026-09-16

`jo/calculators/social-security-pension/index.html` — old-age (تقاعد الشيخوخة) and early-retirement (التقاعد المبكر) pension estimator, complementing the existing `jo/calculators/social-security/` (which computes ongoing *contributions*, not the eventual *pension payout* — these are two different calculators, cross-linked both ways).

**Formula, verified directly against the primary source** (Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 as amended, full text fetched from `ssc.gov.jo`'s own published PDF — not a secondary source): Articles 62 (old-age) and 64 (early retirement), plus the attached Table 5 (early-retirement age-based reduction percentages) and Table 4 (dependents' increment, cross-referenced against Article 62(c)/64(d)(3) directly since Table 4 itself covers survivor-benefit shares, a different topic — the dependents percentages used here come from the article text, not Table 4).

- **Base pension**: 2.5% × years of contribution on the first 1,500 JOD of average monthly wage, + 2% × years of contribution on the excess above 1,500 JOD.
- **Old-age eligibility**: age 60 (male) / 55 (female) + at least 180 contributions. Full base pension, no reduction.
- **Early-retirement eligibility** (the *general* path, Article 64(a) — see scope note below): age ≥50 with ≥252 actual contributions (male) or ≥228 (female), OR age ≥45 with ≥300 actual contributions regardless of gender.
- **Early-retirement reduction**: base pension reduced by an age-bucketed percentage per Table 5 — 20% at age 45 down to 2% at age 59 for men (15 one-year buckets), 14% at 45 down to 2% at 54 for women (10 buckets, since women's normal retirement age is 55).
- **Dependents increment**: +12% of pension for the 1st dependent (min 10, max 100 JOD) + 6% each for a 2nd and 3rd dependent (min 10, max 25 JOD each) — capped at 3 dependents, matches the exact wording in both Article 62(c) and 64(d)(3).

**Deliberately out of scope, flagged transparently in the page's own disclaimer** (this law has extensive grandfather/transitional provisions that would multiply the complexity far beyond what a simple estimator can responsibly cover):
- The *newest* early-retirement path (Article 64(h), for insured persons whose *first-ever* SSC enrollment happened after the current amendment) has stricter conditions (age 55/252 male, 52/228 female) and its *own separate* reduction table embedded in 64(h)(3) — not Table 5. Since most of the current workforce was insured before this amendment, Article 64(a)'s general path (used by this calculator) is the broadly-applicable one, but very recently-first-insured workers should verify their own case.
- Hazardous-profession early retirement (Article 64(c) — different age/contribution thresholds).
- The pre-2014 transitional early-retirement paths (Article 64(b) — depends on the person's exact age *before* the law took effect, now decreasingly relevant with time).
- The anti-wage-spike averaging cap (Articles 62(d)/64(d)(4) — the law caps how much the average-wage calculation can be inflated by a large raise shortly before retirement; this calculator has no way to know the wage 4-5 years prior, so it isn't applied).
- Disability/incapacity pensions (راتب اعتلال) and survivor/death pensions (راتب تقاعد الوفاة) — entirely separate benefit categories under this same law, not covered here.
- Military/police insured persons (Article 71 excludes them — separate system).

**Verification method**: formula and all percentages checked directly against the official law PDF (not a news article or secondary summary) — fetched `ssc.gov.jo`'s own consolidated law text and read Articles 62/64 and Table 5 in full before writing any number. 4 hand-calculated test cases (old-age with 2 dependents, early retirement via the age-50 path, non-eligible at age 40, early retirement via the age-45/300-contribution path for a woman with 1 dependent) all matched the calculator's live output exactly, confirmed via CDP against both a local Fetch-intercepted copy and the final live production page.

**Found and fixed during this build — see the CSP section immediately above**: the calculator initially used `oninput="calc()"` (matching this file family's existing established pattern, e.g. `jo/calculators/social-security/index.html`), which turned out to be silently broken by the site's own enforced CSP. Caught via live CDP testing before considering the page shipped (the live production test showed empty result fields despite 0 thrown JS errors) — converted to `addEventListener('input', calc)` before finalizing.


## Saudi GOSI retirement pension calculator — built 2026-09-16

`sa/calculators/gosi-pension/index.html` — a GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) old-age/early-retirement pension estimator, the Saudi analog of the Jordan pension calculator above, built as idea #2 from a background research fork investigating real-search-demand tool ideas for Jordan/Gulf markets (idea #1, the Jordan pension calculator, was built first the same day; a fresh research fork was launched immediately after this one to regenerate a full ranked idea list, per explicit user instruction — see whatever section documents that fork's outcome for what came of it).

**Formula, verified directly against the primary source**: fetched the actual **Social Insurance Law** (Royal Decree No. M/273, dated 26/12/1445H, effective 3 July 2024) — the official English translation published by the Bureau of Experts at the Council of Ministers (`misa.gov.sa`), not a secondary HR-blog summary (several were checked first via WebSearch and found to disagree with each other on the exact contribution-rate/retirement-age numbers, which is exactly the kind of conflict this project's verification policy exists to catch). Read Part 2 ("Annuities Branch", Articles 14-27) in full.

- **Base pension** (Article 17.1): 2.25% × average contributory wage (highest 180 contribution months, Article 26.1) × months of contribution ÷ 12, capped at 100% of that average wage.
- **Normal retirement** (Article 16.1-2): legal age 65, minimum 180 months (15 years) of contribution — full base pension, subject to the minimum-pension floor below.
- **Minimum pension floor** (Article 17.2): SAR 4,000/month at 480 months (40 years) contribution; SAR 2,000/month at the 180-month minimum; linear interpolation between. Applied only to normal (age-65) retirement in this calculator — see the ambiguity note below.
- **Early retirement** (Article 16.3): available up to 120 months (10 years) before legal age — i.e. as early as 55 — if the contributor has stopped GOSI-covered employment and has at least 360 months (30 years) of contribution.
- **Early-retirement reduction** (Article 17.3): 3% per 12 months, applied not to the full gap-to-65 but to `min(months before legal age, months short of 480)` — i.e., a longer contribution history softens the early-retirement penalty. Confirmed via hand-worked examples before coding (e.g., exactly 30 years contributed retiring at exactly 55 → both gaps are 120 months → full 30% reduction; 35 years retiring at 58 → 84 months early but only 60 months short of 480 → reduction capped at 15%, not 21%).
- **No gender distinction and no dependents increment** — unlike Jordan's law, this Saudi law's retirement-age/reduction rules are gender-neutral and the retiree's own pension amount carries no per-dependent boost while alive (family-member survivor benefits are a separate, later article not covered by this calculator) — so the calculator has no gender toggle and no dependents field, a genuine (not accidental) difference from the Jordan page's inputs.
- **Contributory wage cap**: SAR 45,000/month (Article 8.2) — user-entered wages above this are silently capped in the calculation, with an on-page note explaining why.

**Deliberately out of scope, flagged transparently in the page's own disclaimer** — this law has an even larger transitional-provisions problem than Jordan's, found while reading the Royal Decree's own transitional clauses (not the Law's numbered articles, which only describe the *steady-state* rules used above):
- **The transitional retirement-age schedule** (Royal Decree Clause Fifth): anyone who was already contributing to GOSI before the law's 3 July 2024 effective date, was under 50 Hijri years old then, and had less than 240 months (20 years) of pre-2024 contribution, follows a **sliding retirement age from 58 years 4 months up to 65** depending on their exact age in 2024 (a full lookup table in the decree, not a formula) — this is not a rare edge case, it's the rule that actually governs most of the current workforce for years to come, but computing it needs the person's exact age *in 2024*, not just their current age, so it isn't modeled here.
- **Full carve-out to the old law**: anyone with 240+ months (20 years) of pre-2024 contribution, or already entitled to a pension before the effective date, is excluded from this new law entirely (Royal Decree Clause Second) and stays under the old Social Insurance Law (M/33) / Civil Pension Law — completely different rules this calculator doesn't attempt.
- **Minimum-floor vs. early-retirement-reduction interaction**: Article 17(3) says the reduction applies to "the pension amount provided for in paragraph (1)" (the raw formula), not explicitly to the floored amount from paragraph (2) — the public law text doesn't fully resolve the order of operations, and the answer likely lives in unpublished implementing regulations. This calculator applies the reduction to the raw formula and applies the minimum floor only to normal (non-early) retirement, and says so explicitly rather than guessing which order GOSI's own systems actually use.
- Disability, survivor/death pensions, the gradual 18%→22% contribution-rate phase-in (2024-2028 — doesn't affect the pension payout formula itself, only the paycheck deduction), and the late-retirement +3%/year bonus for working past 65 (Article 17.4) — all separate topics, not modeled.

**Built CSP-safe from the start** (the project has an active, unfixed site-wide bug where enforced CSP silently blocks `oninput`/`onchange` attributes — see the CRITICAL BUG section above): every input on this page uses `element.addEventListener('input', calc)` wired in a `DOMContentLoaded` block, no inline event-handler attributes anywhere — confirmed 0 via a `<tag ...on(click|input|change)=` regex sweep of the finished file, and confirmed live via a `document.addEventListener('securitypolicyviolation', ...)` listener returning an empty array after real `input` events were dispatched on the live production page.

**Verification**: 8 hand-calculated test cases (normal retirement at various contribution lengths, early retirement at the max-reduction boundary and a softened-reduction case, sub-180-month lump-sum-only case, not-yet-eligible cases for both the "wait to 65" and "need 360 months + stop working" branches, floor-applies and floor-doesn't-apply cases) run against a standalone Node reproduction of the exact formula before ever touching the HTML — all 8 matched hand derivation exactly. Then re-verified live via CDP against the pushed production page (not just local files): filled real inputs, dispatched real `input` events, read the rendered result back out of the DOM for the normal-retirement, early-retirement, and lump-sum-ineligible cases, plus the language toggle — all matched the standalone reproduction's numbers exactly, 0 CSP violations, 0 JS errors. `test-calculators.js` (20/20) and `test-e2e.js` (29/29) re-run clean afterward as a broader regression check, since this build also touched the shared `i18n.js` (2 new additive keys) and `sa/index.html`.

**Integration**: added to `sa/index.html`'s `decisionsSection` grid (alongside car-cost/wedding-cost/university-cost), cross-linked to `sa/calculators/car-cost/`, `sa/vat-calculator/`, `sa/gold-price/`; added to `build-sitemap.js`/`sitemap.xml` (now 303 URLs) and `build-search-index.js`/`search-index.json` (137 entries). Self-contained per-page AR/EN toggle (`toggleLang()`/`LANG_AR`/`LANG_EN`), matching the established pattern for `sa/calculators/car-cost/` and the rest of the country-module family — not wired into `i18n.js`'s global translation system, except for the two new hub-card keys (`sa_gosi_title`/`sa_gosi_desc`, ar+en only) needed so the hub card itself doesn't fall into the mixed-language bug class already fixed extensively elsewhere in this file.


## UAE and Saudi end-of-service gratuity calculators — built 2026-09-16

`ae/calculators/end-of-service/index.html` and `sa/calculators/end-of-service/index.html` — items #1 and #2 from the fresh research fork's ranked idea list (both flagged there as the best-ROI candidates: strong demand evidence, small-medium effort, minimal regulatory ambiguity). Built together per explicit user instruction ("Both").

**UAE formula, verified via WebSearch cross-referencing multiple independent sources** (a UAE government platform page 404'd when fetched directly, so this relied on corroboration across several detailed, article-citing secondary sources — Khaleej Times, Bayzat, BCL Globiz, Access Financial, edarabia, gulfnews — all of which agreed exactly on every number, a strong signal for a decades-simpler law than Saudi's GOSI reform): Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, Article 51.
- Daily wage = basic monthly salary ÷ 30.
- Gratuity = 21 days × daily wage × min(years, 5) + 30 days × daily wage × max(years − 5, 0). Fractions of a year pro-rated.
- **Hard cutoff, not pro-rated**: under 1 year of continuous service = zero gratuity (unlike Oman's Article 61, which pro-rates even sub-1-year service — a genuine difference between the two laws' 2021-era reforms, worth remembering if these get confused).
- **The 2021 law abolished the old resignation penalty** — resignation and termination now get the identical amount (multiple sources independently confirmed this), so unlike the Saudi calculator below, this page has no reason/resignation toggle at all.
- Capped at 24× basic monthly salary (2 years' basic wage) — rarely binds in practice (needs ~29+ years of continuous service under the standard formula before it does), but implemented and hand-verified anyway (a 40-year/SAR-5000 test case confirmed the cap correctly engages).
- Excluded, disclosed transparently: free-zone-specific rules (which can differ from mainland MOHRE rules), civil-service/government employment (not covered by this law at all), and gross-misconduct dismissal under Article 44 (can forfeit gratuity entirely).

**Saudi formula, verified via WebSearch** (multiple independent, article-citing sources agreed exactly — this is a stable, decades-old law unlike the new GOSI reform, so cross-referenced secondary sources were treated as sufficiently reliable without chasing the primary Arabic legal text): Labor Law (Royal Decree M/51), Articles 84 and 85.
- Base benefit (Article 84): half a month's wage × min(years, 5) + a full month's wage × max(years − 5, 0), based on the last wage.
- **Termination/contract-end**: full Article 84 amount, no reduction.
- **Resignation** (Article 85) applies a service-length-tiered multiplier instead: 0% under 2 years, 1/3 from 2–5 years, 2/3 from 5–10 years, 100% at 10+ years — this is why, unlike the UAE page, this calculator has a reason toggle (termination vs. resignation) that materially changes the result.
- Excluded, disclosed transparently: disciplinary dismissal (Article 80, can forfeit the benefit) and the Article 81 exception where certain resignation reasons grant the full amount despite short tenure.

**Built CSP-safe from the start**, same discipline as the GOSI pension calculator above — `addEventListener` only, 0 inline event-handler attributes on either page, confirmed via regex sweep before pushing.

**Verification**: 4 UAE + 4 Saudi hand-calculated test cases run against a standalone Node reproduction of each formula before touching the HTML (UAE: a sub-5-year case, an above-5-year case matching a cited worked example exactly, a cap-triggering extreme-tenure case, and a sub-1-year ineligibility case; Saudi: a resignation case in the 5-10-year two-thirds tier, a resignation case past the 10-year full-entitlement threshold, a sub-2-year resignation ineligibility case, and a termination case showing no reduction applies) — all 8 matched exactly. Re-verified live via CDP against the pushed production pages: real `input` events dispatched, results read back from the DOM, matched the standalone reproduction exactly on all 6 live-tested cases, 0 CSP violations, 0 JS errors on either page.

**Integration**: added to `ae/index.html`'s and `sa/index.html`'s `decisionsSection` grids, cross-linked to each country's car-cost/VAT/gold-price tools (and to `sa/calculators/gosi-pension/` from the Saudi gratuity page, and vice versa), new `ae_gratuity_title`/`desc` and `sa_gratuity_title`/`desc` i18n keys (ar+en only, matching the established scope for this hub-card family). `build-sitemap.js`/`sitemap.xml` now 305 URLs, `search-index.json` 139 entries.

## Jordan pension calculator redesign — project early vs. normal retirement, 2026-09-16

User feedback (Jordanian dialect, casual): suggested adding "your expected pension" by asking current salary + expected salary at end of service for early or normal retirement. Investigated first rather than building the literal ask: the law's "average wage" base is specifically the **last 36 contributions (3 years) for normal retirement, or last 60 (5 years) for early** — not a lifetime average — so averaging "current salary" (which could be decades away from retirement) with "expected salary at retirement" would often produce a misleading number for anyone not already close to retiring. Proposed instead: a single field asking for expected wage specifically **near** retirement (the number that actually matters legally), and — since the user's own phrasing explicitly named both paths ("تقاعد مبكر او طبيعي") — projecting **both** early and normal retirement forward from the person's *current* age/contributions, rather than requiring them to already know a hypothetical target retirement age. Confirmed with the user before building ("طيب تمام" / "ابني ب فكرتك").

**Redesigned `jo/calculators/social-security-pension/index.html` inputs**: `عمرك عند التقاعد` (age at retirement) → `عمرك الحالي` (current age); `متوسط أجرك الشهري` (average wage) → `راتبك المتوقع بالسنين الأخيرة قبل التقاعد` (expected wage near retirement) — with the note explaining why (matches the 3-year/5-year legal averaging window, not a lifetime or current-salary average). Gender toggle and dependents field unchanged.

**New projection logic** (`calc()` rewritten): given current age + current contribution-months, computes the **earliest possible early-retirement age** by projecting continuous future contribution forward against both Article 64(a) paths (age≥50 + 252/228 contributions, or age≥45 + 300 contributions) — takes the earlier-qualifying of the two paths — then applies the existing age-bucketed reduction table at that projected age. Separately computes the **normal retirement** projection at the legal age (60/55), with contributions projected forward the same way. Both scenarios render as separate cards when they apply; if projected contributions at normal retirement age still fall short of the 180-contribution minimum, shows a single not-eligible message with the actual projected shortfall instead of either card. **A newly-disclosed assumption, added to the page's own "what this doesn't cover" list**: the projection assumes continuous, uninterrupted future contribution from the entered current age, at a wage matching the entered near-retirement figure — a real gap in someone's contribution history or a materially different final wage will change the real outcome.

**Verification**: 4 hand-calculated scenarios (a 35-year-old male with a 10-year head start, a 40-year-old female with dependents, a 52-year-old male already past the early-eligibility contribution threshold, and a 58-year-old male too late-starting to ever qualify under either path) run against a standalone Node reproduction of the exact new projection formula before touching the HTML — all 4 matched by hand. Re-verified live via CDP against the pushed production page with the identical 4 scenarios (including a live gender-toggle click for the female case) — all matched exactly, 0 CSP violations, 0 JS errors.

**Also added, matching the pattern just shipped for `jo/calculators/social-security/`**: an `exportPensionResult()` function + `#pExportBtn` using the shared `exportResultImage()` canvas utility — rows show the entered near-retirement wage plus whichever of the early/normal results actually rendered (the normal-retirement row is the highlighted one when both are shown, since it's the guaranteed unreduced figure; the early row is highlighted only when it's the sole scenario shown).
