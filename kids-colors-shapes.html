❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What will my child learn here?

Twelve basic colors, twelve shapes, the numbers 1 to 20 and common school words such as book, pencil and bag. Each one is shown clearly and read aloud in English.

Do the numbers show the quantity too?

Yes. Each number card shows the digit, the English word and a row of dots so the child connects the symbol, the sound and the amount.

Can we practise without reading?

Yes. In Quiz mode the voice says a word and the child taps the matching color, shape, number or object, so no reading is needed.

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents & teachers

What your child learns

Colors, shapes and numbers are the first concepts in most KG English programs. Seeing the color or shape while hearing its English name helps children build vocabulary they can use straight away — “a red circle”, “three apples”.

Tips for parents

Turn it into a game: ask your child to find something blue in the room, count the steps on the stairs in English, or spot a triangle on the way to school. Revisit the same group a few days in a row before moving on.

All Adawati kids games are free, need no sign-up and run in the browser. Pictures are simple emoji and original drawings. Screen time is best kept short and shared — playing together and talking about the answers helps children learn more.
