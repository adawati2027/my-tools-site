❓ FAQ
What's the average cost of college in the US for 2025-26?

Per College Board's 2025-26 Trends in College Pricing report: public 4-year in-state averages $11,950 tuition & fees ($29,910 total cost of attendance with room & board). Public 4-year out-of-state averages $31,880 tuition & fees ($49,080 total). Private nonprofit 4-year averages $45,000 tuition & fees ($62,570 total).

Why is out-of-state tuition so much higher?

Public universities are subsidized by state tax dollars for residents of that state. Out-of-state and international students don't pay those state taxes, so they're charged closer to the full cost of instruction — often nearly 3x the in-state rate.
