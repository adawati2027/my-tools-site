❓ FAQ
Should I call 911 or 988?

Call 911 for any situation with immediate physical danger — crime, fire, medical emergency. Call or text 988 for a mental health or suicide crisis when there's no immediate physical danger; trained counselors are available 24/7.

Does 911 work without a phone plan?

Yes — 911 works from any phone in the US, even one without an active carrier plan or SIM card.
