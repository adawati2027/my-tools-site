💡 Getting the Most Out of Weather Forecasts

Forecast accuracy drops the further out you look — today and tomorrow's forecasts are the most reliable, while day 4 and 5 are closer to a general trend than precise numbers. The "feels like" temperature differs from the actual air temperature because it factors in humidity and wind speed, and that gap becomes more noticeable in humid or windy conditions.

For daily planning, check the hour-by-hour breakdown instead of relying on the single daily number — especially useful if you're commuting or planning outdoor activities at a specific time. The United States spans many climate zones, so conditions can vary dramatically between regions on the same day — always check your specific city rather than assuming a national trend.
