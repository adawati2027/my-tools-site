Delete Your Account

Adawati (adawati.space and the Adawati Android & iOS app)

Delete it yourself, in under a minute

  1. Open Adawati (website or app) and sign in.
  2. Tap your name in the top bar (the 👤 button).
  3. Tap Delete my account (small red link under Sign Out).
  4. Confirm with your password (or with Google, if you signed in with Google) and tap Delete permanently.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address, display name, profile photo, password credential).
  • Your favorites and recently-used tools list.
  • Tool data saved to your account (to-do list, habit tracker, shopping list, expense splitter, savings goal, event countdowns and similar).
  • Any ads-board listings you posted.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Data stored only on your own device (browser storage) stays on your device until you clear it. Aggregated, anonymous analytics are not linked to your account.

Can't sign in?

Email ahmed.amawi@outlook.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Adawati account". We will delete the account and its data manually, normally within 7 days.
