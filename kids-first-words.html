❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What ages is this English page for?

It is made for kindergarten (KG) children, roughly ages 3 to 7, who are starting to learn English. Every word has a picture and a spoken voice, so children who cannot read yet can still learn.

How does the sound work?

Tap any picture and your device reads the word aloud in English. The “Slow” button repeats it more slowly, and “Play all” reads the whole group one word after another. The voice comes from your own phone or computer.

I cannot hear any sound. What should I check?

Make sure the volume is up and the phone is not in silent mode. Some browsers need one tap on the page before they will play speech. If it is still silent, try Chrome or Safari — the page uses the voice that is built into your device.

What topics are included?

Parts of the body, wild animals, pets and farm animals, birds, sea animals, insects, flowers, fruits, vegetables and vehicles. Colors, shapes and numbers are on a separate page.

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents & teachers

What your child learns

Children learn words fastest when they see, hear and say them together. Each card shows a picture with the spoken word, grouped by topic the way many KG English books do, so a child can move from “nose” and “hand” to “lion”, “apple” and “bus” in small, friendly steps.

Tips for parents

Keep each session short (5–10 minutes) and pick one topic a day. Ask your child to say the word after the voice, point to the real thing at home or in the street, and use “Play all” as a warm-up before the quiz. Praise tries, not only correct answers.

All Adawati kids games are free, need no sign-up and run in the browser. Pictures are simple emoji and original drawings. Screen time is best kept short and shared — playing together and talking about the answers helps children learn more.
