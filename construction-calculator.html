❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How much paint do I need per square meter?

On a previously painted wall, 1 liter of paint typically covers about 10 square meters per coat. On bare or newly plastered walls the first coat covers less, around 6-8 square meters per liter, since the surface absorbs more paint. Two coats is standard practice for an even, durable finish.

How much wastage should I add for tiles?

For a simple straight (grid) layout, add about 10% extra tiles to cover cutting waste and breakage. For diagonal layouts or complex patterns like herringbone, increase this to 15-20% since more cutting is involved.

How many bags of cement do I need per cubic meter of concrete?

For M15 mix (1:2:4 cement:sand:aggregate), roughly 6.34 bags (50kg each) of cement are needed per cubic meter. For M20 mix (1:1.5:3), roughly 8.06 bags per cubic meter. These figures already account for the standard 1.54 dry-to-wet volume conversion factor.

What is the difference between M15 and M20 concrete?

M15 and M20 refer to the concrete's mix ratio and compressive strength grade (15 and 20 N/mm² respectively). M15 (1:2:4 cement:sand:aggregate) suits non-structural work like flooring base or pathways. M20 (1:1.5:3) is stronger and more commonly used for structural elements like foundations, columns and slabs. Always confirm the right grade with a qualified engineer for load-bearing work.

Is this calculator accurate enough for a real construction project?

This tool gives a rough DIY-level estimate using standard industry coverage rates and mix ratios, useful for budgeting and shopping lists. It is not a substitute for a professional engineer's calculation, especially for structural concrete work — actual material needs vary by brand, surface condition, wastage in practice, and local building codes.

Planning Paint, Tile and Concrete Quantities

Buying too little material means a mid-project run to the store (and often a slightly different batch/shade); buying too much wastes money on leftovers. This calculator uses standard, widely-cited industry coverage rates and mix ratios to give a sensible middle estimate — always with a small safety buffer built in.

Worked example: painting a 40 m² room, 2 coats, previously painted wall

40 m² × 2 coats ÷ 10 m²/L = 8 liters, plus a 10% buffer ≈ 8.8 liters — round up to the nearest can size available.

Worked example: a 3m × 4m slab, 10cm thick, M20 mix

Volume = 3 × 4 × 0.10 = 1.2 m³. At ~8.06 bags/m³ for M20, that's 1.2 × 8.06 ≈ 9.67 bags, plus 5% buffer ≈ 10.15 — round up to 11 bags of cement (50kg each).
