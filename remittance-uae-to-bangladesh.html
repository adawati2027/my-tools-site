Send Money from UAE to Bangladesh

What to prepare, the 2.5% incentive, and how to compare providers

This guide covers the practical steps for sending money from the UAE to Bangladesh. It does not list fees or exchange rates, because those change daily.

What to prepare before you send

  • Your passport and Emirates ID. Providers usually ask for both.
  • The recipient's full name exactly as it appears on their Bangladeshi bank account or wallet.
  • The recipient's bank account number, or the mobile wallet number, if the provider pays out to wallets.
  • The amount you want to send, so you can get a quote for that exact amount.

Using a licensed provider

Exchange houses and remittance providers in the UAE must be licensed by the Central Bank of the UAE. Use a licensed provider, because the 2.5% incentive described below only applies to remittances through official channels.

The 2.5% government incentive

Press coverage reports that Bangladesh pays a 2.5% cash incentive on remittances sent through official channels. We could not confirm the current terms against the official text, so check how it is applied with your provider and with Bangladesh Bank before you rely on it.

How to compare providers

Compare the amount the recipient actually receives in taka, not only the fee. Ask each provider for a quote for the same amount on the same day, then enter the fee and exchange rate into the Remittance Comparison Calculator.

Be careful with offers that say "no fee". The exchange rate can carry a margin that costs more than a small fixed fee.

Delivery options

Options include a bank account, and for some providers a mobile wallet such as bKash or Nagad. Speed and options depend on the provider, so confirm them before you send.

After you send

Keep the transfer reference and receipt. If the money has not arrived when the provider says it should, contact the provider with the reference first.

Verification note: This guide was compiled in October 2026 from provider help pages, industry guides and press coverage. The Bangladesh Bank and Central Bank of the UAE websites could not be checked directly during this review, so the incentive terms and licensing points above have not yet been confirmed against the official texts. Rules, incentive terms and provider services change. Confirm with the regulator and your provider before sending money.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Does the 2.5% incentive apply to every transfer?

Press coverage reports that the incentive applies to remittances sent through official channels, not informal ones. The exact conditions were not confirmed against the official text, so check with your provider before you send.

Does this guide show the current AED to BDT rate?

No. Rates and fees change often and differ by provider and amount. Get a quote for the amount you plan to send from each provider, then compare them with the Remittance Comparison Calculator.

Can the money go to a mobile wallet?

Some providers can pay out to mobile wallets such as bKash or Nagad, and others only pay to bank accounts. Check the delivery options your provider offers for the recipient before you send.
