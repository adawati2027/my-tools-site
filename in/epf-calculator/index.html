📌 Current EPF Rules (as of September 2026)
  • Employee contribution: 12% of Basic+DA (10% for establishments with under 20 employees)
  • Employer contribution: 12% total — split into 3.67% (EPF) + 8.33% (EPS, capped)
  • Wage ceiling for mandatory contribution: ₹25,000/month (raised from ₹15,000, effective 17 Sep 2026)
  • Maximum EPS contribution: ₹2,083/month (8.33% × ₹25,000 ceiling)
  • Current EPF interest rate: 8.25% p.a. for FY 2025-26 — declared fresh each financial year, can change
  • Employer also pays 0.5% EDLI insurance (capped ₹75/employee) and 0.5% admin charges — these don't reduce your take-home or your own EPF balance

Official reference: Employees' Provident Fund Organisation — epfindia.gov.in

⚠️ Important — Read Before Relying on This

This is an estimate only, not an official EPFO calculation. It assumes a flat salary (no yearly increments), a flat 8.25% interest rate for the entire period (the real rate is set annually and will change), and it does not model the EPS pension payout, partial withdrawals, or job changes. For your exact, authoritative balance, check your EPF passbook at passbook.epfindia.gov.in or the UMANG app.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is the current EPF contribution rate?

The employee contributes 12% of Basic+DA to EPF (10% for establishments with fewer than 20 employees). The employer also contributes 12% total, but it is split: 3.67% goes to the EPF account and 8.33% goes to the EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme), subject to a cap.

What is the new EPF wage ceiling?

Effective 17 September 2026, the mandatory EPF wage ceiling was raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month (Basic+DA), under the new Code on Social Security 2020 framework. Contribution on any amount above ₹25,000 is voluntary, not mandatory.

What is the current EPF interest rate?

EPFO declared 8.25% per annum for FY 2025-26, ratified by the Central Board of Trustees in March 2026. This rate is declared fresh every financial year and can change — it is not a guaranteed fixed rate for the future.

What is the EPS contribution cap?

The employer's EPS (pension) contribution is 8.33% of Basic+DA, capped at the wage ceiling. With the new ₹25,000 ceiling, the maximum monthly EPS contribution is ₹2,083 (8.33% × ₹25,000), up from ₹1,250 under the old ₹15,000 ceiling.

Does this calculator include EPS pension value?

No. EPS contributions build toward a monthly pension paid after retirement, not a lump-sum corpus, so this calculator's projected corpus only includes the employee's EPF share plus the employer's 3.67% EPF share — not the EPS portion, which is shown separately as a monthly figure only.
