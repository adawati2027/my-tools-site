FAQ — Bangladeshi Workers in Oman

Do Bangladeshi expats in Oman pay SPF deductions?

No. The Social Protection Fund (SPF) 8% deduction applies only to Omani nationals. Bangladeshi and other expatriate workers keep their full gross salary with no SPF deduction.

How is end-of-service gratuity calculated for Bangladeshis in Oman?

Under Article 61 of Oman Labour Law: 1 month basic salary per year of service, with partial years pro-rated. Example: 5 years at OMR 350 basic salary = OMR 1,750 gratuity. Use the End of Service Calculator for your exact figure.

Is there income tax on salary in Oman for Bangladeshi workers?

As of 2026, Oman does not impose personal income tax on salaries. A Personal Income Tax law was enacted in 2025 but is scheduled to take effect from 2028. Currently no tax deduction applies to Bangladeshi workers' salaries in Oman.

What is the best way to send money from Oman to Bangladesh?

Popular services include: bank wire transfers, exchange houses (Al Ansari, Western Union), and online platforms (bKash-compatible services, Wise, Remitly). Compare rates before sending. Use the OMR to BDT Currency Converter to check the live rate.

What labour rights do Bangladeshi workers have in Oman?

Oman Labour Law protects all workers regardless of nationality. Key rights: end-of-service gratuity (Article 61), 1 month notice period, 30 days annual leave after 1 year of service, and the right to file a complaint at the Ministry of Labour if your employer violates your contract.
