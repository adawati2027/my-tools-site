Send Money from Oman to India

What to prepare, how the rules work, and how to compare providers

Last reviewed October 2026. This guide covers the practical steps for sending money from Oman to a bank account in India. It does not list fees or exchange rates, because those change daily.

What to prepare before you send

  • Your passport and Omani residence card. Providers usually ask for both, so have them with you when you go to an exchange house or sign up to an app.
  • The recipient's full name exactly as it appears on their Indian bank account.
  • The recipient's bank account number and IFSC code for a bank transfer.
  • The amount you want to send, so you can get a quote for that exact amount.

How the rules work

In Oman, money transfer services are licensed and supervised by the Central Bank of Oman. Use a licensed exchange house or bank, not an informal channel.

In India, incoming foreign remittances are handled by authorised banks under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA). The Reserve Bank of India publishes the rules. Your recipient's bank is the one that credits the money to the account.

How to compare providers

Look at the amount the recipient actually gets, not the fee alone. Ask each provider for a quote for the same amount on the same day, then enter the fee and exchange rate into the Remittance Comparison Calculator. It shows which option delivers more.

Be careful with offers that say "no fee". The exchange rate can carry a margin that costs more than a small fixed fee.

Delivery options

Most providers credit a bank account in India, often through NEFT or IMPS. Some also offer cash pickup or a wallet. Speed and options depend on the provider, so check what is offered before you send.

After you send

Keep the transfer reference and receipt. If the money has not arrived when the provider says it should, contact the provider with the reference first.

Verification note: This guide was compiled in October 2026 from provider help pages, industry guides and press coverage. The Central Bank of Oman and Reserve Bank of India websites could not be checked directly during this review, so the regulatory points above have not yet been confirmed against the official texts. Rules, licensing and provider services change. Confirm with the regulator and your provider before sending money.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Does this guide show the current exchange rate?

No. Exchange rates and fees change often and differ by provider, amount and payment method. Get the quote from each provider for the amount you plan to send, then compare them with the Remittance Comparison Calculator.

Why does the name on the transfer matter?

Indian banks credit the money to the account number. A name that does not match the account holder can delay or block the payment, so enter the name exactly as it appears on the recipient's bank account.

Should I choose the lowest fee?

Not necessarily. A provider with no fee can still give a weaker exchange rate than one with a small fee. Compare the amount the recipient actually receives.
