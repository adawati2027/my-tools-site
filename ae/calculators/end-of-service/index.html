❓ FAQ
How is gratuity calculated?

21 days of basic salary per year for the first 5 years, and 30 days per year after that. Daily rate = monthly basic salary ÷ 30. Fractions of a year are pro-rated.

Do I still get it if I resign?

Yes — the 2021 law removed the old resignation penalty. Resigning and being terminated now get the same amount, as long as you completed at least 1 year of service (except gross-misconduct dismissal).

What if I worked less than a year?

No gratuity at all — 1 year of continuous service is a hard minimum, not pro-rated below that.

Is there a maximum?

Yes, gratuity is capped at 2 years' basic salary total, regardless of service length.

Basic salary or total salary?

Basic salary only, as stated in your MOHRE contract — housing, transport, bonuses and overtime are excluded.
