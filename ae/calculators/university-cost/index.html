❓ FAQ
How much does a credit hour cost at UAE universities?

Private university credit-hour rates commonly range from about AED 800 to AED 2,900+ depending on the institution and program (business/humanities are usually cheaper than engineering or medicine). Total annual private undergraduate tuition often falls between AED 50,000 and AED 150,000+. Check your specific university's official fee schedule for the exact rate.

What other costs should I add besides tuition?

Registration/admission fees, books and course materials, housing (especially in Dubai/Abu Dhabi if you're not living with family), transport, and health insurance (often mandatory for student visas). These can add a meaningful amount on top of tuition, especially for international students needing accommodation.

📌 Source & disclaimer
