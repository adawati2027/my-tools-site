❓ FAQ
Does 112 work in the UAE?

Yes — 112 works across the UAE as a backup emergency number and connects to the same emergency services as 999.

Is this list complete?

No, these are just the 3 core emergency numbers. For the full list (Coast Guard, non-emergency police lines, utilities, etc.) check the official government link above.

What is the police number in the UAE?

999 is the official police emergency number across the UAE, toll-free and available 24/7 from any phone.

What is the ambulance number in the UAE?

998 connects you to ambulance services anywhere in the UAE.

What is the fire/civil defence number in the UAE?

997 is the official number for fire emergencies and Civil Defence across the UAE.
