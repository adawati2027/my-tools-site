Free Online Tools for UAE Residents

Whether you're in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, or anywhere across the Emirates, these 40+ free tools and guides cover the calculations you run into every day — from splitting a restaurant bill to calculating your loan installment or checking your BMI.

VAT Calculator for UAE (5%)

The UAE introduced Value Added Tax at 5% on 1 January 2018. The VAT calculator above lets you add VAT to any price or strip it out (reverse VAT). Just enter the amount and choose whether to add or remove VAT — result appears instantly.

AED Currency Converter

The UAE Dirham (AED) has been pegged to the US Dollar at 3.6725 since 1997. The currency converter uses live rates from an external API updated every 4 hours for non-AED pairs (EUR, GBP, INR, SAR, OMR, and 25+ others).

Tip Calculator for Restaurants

Many UAE restaurants add a 10% service charge automatically. If yours doesn't, the tip calculator lets you choose your tip percentage, add the service charge, and split the final amount between any number of people — each person's share appears instantly.
