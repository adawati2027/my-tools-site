Free Embeddable Widgets

Add a live gold price or a UAE VAT calculator to your website with one line of code

Copy the code, paste it into your page, and the widget works. No sign-up and no script to install. Each widget includes a small "Powered by Adawati" link, please keep it.

1. Gold price per gram

Shows the 24K price per gram, with 21K and 18K calculated from it. The visitor can switch currency. Add ?currency= to set the default (USD, JOD, AED, SAR, OMR, KWD, QAR, BHD, EGP, EUR, GBP).

<iframe src="https://adawati.space/embed/gold/?currency=JOD" width="100%" height="270" style="border:0;max-width:420px" loading="lazy" title="Gold price per gram"></iframe>

2. UAE VAT calculator (5%)

Adds or removes 5% VAT from an amount in AED.

<iframe src="https://adawati.space/embed/uae-vat/" width="100%" height="330" style="border:0;max-width:420px" loading="lazy" title="UAE VAT calculator"></iframe>

Good to know

  • The gold price is the international spot price, not a local market price. The widget says so.
  • The VAT widget is an estimate. Check the rules with the UAE Federal Tax Authority.
  • You can change the width and height to fit your layout. The widgets follow the visitor's light or dark mode.
  • Need the raw numbers instead? See the public API.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Are the widgets free?

Yes. Paste the iframe code on your site. The only condition is keeping the small Powered by Adawati link inside the widget.

Is the gold price a local market price?

No. It is the international XAU/USD spot price converted to the chosen currency. Local dealers add their own margins.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Add ?currency=JOD (or another supported code) to the widget address to set the default currency.
