❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. All compression happens directly inside your browser. Your video never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server.

How much can I reduce the file size?

It depends heavily on the video content. Screen recordings and videos with large static areas can shrink by 90% or more. Regular handheld footage with a lot of motion typically shrinks by 50-80%.

Why does compression take time?

The browser re-encodes the video frame by frame in real time while it plays, so processing takes roughly as long as the video itself. There is no server doing the work faster in the background.

What output format will I get?

The tool automatically picks the best format your browser supports for recording — usually MP4 on Safari/iOS and WebM on Chrome, Firefox and Edge.

Video Compressor — Reduce Size Without a Server

This tool compresses video directly in your browser using the Canvas API to redraw each frame at a smaller size, and the MediaRecorder API to re-encode the result at a lower bitrate. No file is ever uploaded — everything happens on your device, which also means there's no file-size upload limit.

Choosing width and bitrate

480px width with 800 kbps is a good starting point for sharing clips on WhatsApp or similar apps. Lower both for screen recordings or long videos where small size matters more than sharpness. Raise both for videos with a lot of movement or fine detail.
