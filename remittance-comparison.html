❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Why doesn't this tool show live exchange rates for each provider automatically?

Remittance fees and exchange rates change daily, sometimes multiple times a day, and differ by corridor (which two countries), amount, and payment method. Publishing a static table would go stale within days and could mislead people into a worse deal. Instead, open each provider's own app or website, get their current quote for your exact amount, and enter it here for an honest side-by-side comparison.

What is the difference between the exchange rate and the fee?

The fee is a flat or percentage charge taken upfront, shown separately. The exchange rate is how much of the destination currency each unit of your sending currency converts to. Some providers advertise "no fee" but quietly use a worse exchange rate (a hidden margin) — this tool accounts for both, since the only number that matters is how much the recipient actually gets.

How do I find the fee and rate to enter for each provider?

Open the provider's app, website, or ask at the exchange house counter, and enter the exact amount you plan to send. Most providers show the fee and the exact exchange rate (or the final receive amount) before you confirm — use those numbers here.

Does this tool track or store which provider I use?

No. All calculation happens in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to Adawati's servers or stored anywhere — refreshing the page clears everything.

Comparing Money Transfer Providers the Right Way

The advertised fee is only half the story. A provider with a $0 fee but a exchange rate 3% worse than the mid-market rate can cost you more than a provider charging a small flat fee with a rate close to the real market rate. The only number that actually matters is how much money arrives in the recipient's hand — which is exactly what this calculator compares.

Worked example

Sending 1,000 of your currency: Provider A charges a 15 fee and offers a rate of 1.41 → (1,000 − 15) × 1.41 = 1,389 received. Provider B charges a 0 fee but a worse rate of 1.35 → 1,000 × 1.35 = 1,350 received. Despite the "free" transfer, Provider B actually delivers 39 less — Provider A is the better deal here.
