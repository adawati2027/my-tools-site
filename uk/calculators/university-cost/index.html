❓ FAQ
What is the UK tuition fee cap for home students in 2026-27?

The maximum tuition fee for home (UK) undergraduate students in England for 2026-27 is £9,790, up from £9,535 in 2025-26 — the first rise since 2017. It's expected to reach £10,050 in 2027-28, then track inflation. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland have separate arrangements for their own home students.

How much do international students pay?

International tuition varies widely — roughly £10,000 to £70,500+/year depending on university and course, with medicine and elite universities at the top end. £15,000-£25,000/year is typical for most non-medical courses.

📌 Source & disclaimer

The £9,790 home-fee cap for 2026-27 and the maintenance loan figures are based on UK government/Student Finance England announcements. This is an estimate for planning purposes only — check gov.uk/student-finance and your specific university offer for exact figures.
