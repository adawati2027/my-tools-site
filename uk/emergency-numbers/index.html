❓ FAQ
999 or 112 — which should I call?

Both work identically in the UK and reach the same police/fire/ambulance/coastguard services — 999 is the traditional UK number, 112 is the pan-European equivalent. Use whichever you remember.

Is 101 free to call?

No — 101 costs a small flat fee per call (typically around 15p), unlike 999/112/111 which are all free.

What is the emergency number in the UK?

999 is the traditional UK emergency number for police, fire, ambulance and coastguard. 112 works identically and reaches the same services — both are free from any phone.

When should I call 101 instead of 999?

Call 101 for non-urgent police matters, like reporting a crime that already happened or a lost item. It's available 24/7 UK-wide and costs a small flat fee per call. Call 999 only when there's an immediate danger to life or a crime in progress.

What is 111 for?

111 is the NHS non-emergency medical helpline — call it for urgent but non-life-threatening health concerns when you're not sure what to do. It's free from any phone.
